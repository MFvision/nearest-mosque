Your next prayer, wherever you are.

Somewhere on Earth, the Adhan is being called right now. Prayer times, the Qibla and the nearest mosque for wherever you are, in one calm app.

Qibla

From anywhere, one direction.

Turn your phone and the arrow leads you to the Kaaba. When you face it, the Qibla lights up and you feel a gentle vibration.

  • Bearing and distance to the Kaaba
  • Without a compass, it is drawn from north and says so

Prayer times

Your next prayer, on the sky of the hour.

The header shows the next prayer and the time left. Its sky turns pink at Fajr, gold at Asr and violet at Maghrib, and if you like, it shows your city’s weather too.

  • 13,090 cities built in, and 11 calculation methods suggested for your country
  • Today’s six times, each with its own bell, and the Hijri date
  • Sky follows the weather: off until you turn it on; then the city’s location, rounded to about 1 km, goes to MET Norway

Weather: based on data from MET Norway

Nearest mosque

See which way the mosque is, and how far.

The compass shows each mosque at its real direction. Switch to the map for walking time and directions.

  • Directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps
  • Built-in mosque data, plus Apple Maps when online search is on

Ask and the library

Ask about Islam and see the source of every answer.

Answers quote the books on your phone and list each source. A scholar’s fatwa comes first when one matches. Behind them are 99 classical books you can read offline.

  • Written on your phone by Apple’s on-device model. Questions never go to a cloud AI.
  • 25 common questions in 22 languages, labelled as awaiting scholar review
  • Creed, Hadith, Fiqh, Tafsir and Sirah, each volume checked before it opens

Answers quote books. They are not a fatwa.

Adhan, reminders and widgets

The Adhan from Makkah or Madinah, and the time left at a glance.

Choose the Adhan of Masjid al-Haram or al-Masjid an-Nabawi for each prayer, with a reminder 10 or 5 minutes before. The countdown waits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

  • A Fajr alarm, and Friday reminders for Al-Kahf and Jumu’ah
  • Silent during prayer: Do Not Disturb on Android; on iPhone, a Shortcut you run with one tap from the Adhan notification
  • Ten kinds of widgets, and Siri tells you the next prayer

Every screen

On every screen you pray with.

One listing for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Vision Pro and Apple Watch, and the same prayer times and Qibla on Android, Windows and Linux.

  • Apple Watch: the next prayer, the Qibla with a tap on your wrist, and tasbih on the Digital Crown
  • 36 interface languages, 6 of them right to left
  • Android on Google Play; Windows and Linux downloaded from this page

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking.

Prayer times, the Qibla and your answers are worked out on your device. There is no account to make and nothing about you is sent to us.

  • Prayer times and Qibla work offline
  • Online only for mosque search (with your location rounded to about 1 km), maps, the downloads you choose and the weather sky if you turn it on

Everything inside

What the app does today, device by device.

Prayer times

  • Next prayer, countdown and the sky of the hour
  • Today’s six times with a bell for each
  • 11 calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, high-latitude rules
  • Minute adjustments for each time and a Hijri adjustment
  • Estimated times where the sun does not set or rise

Sky follows the weather

  • Clouds, rain, thunderstorms, fog and snow on the sky of the hour
  • The temperature and the wind, with an icon for the weather
  • Off until you turn it on; then the city’s location, rounded to about 1 km, goes to MET Norway
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro, Android, Windows and Linux

Qibla

  • Live compass that lights up and taps when you face the Kaaba
  • Bearing and distance to the Kaaba
  • Drawn from north on devices without a compass

Nearest mosque

  • Compass radar with each mosque at its real direction
  • Map with walking time, calls and websites
  • Directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps
  • Built-in mosque data, plus Apple Maps when online search is on

Ask and the library

  • Answers quoting books on your phone, each with its source
  • A scholar’s fatwa first when one matches
  • 25 common questions in 22 languages, awaiting scholar review
  • 99 classical books in 450 volumes, read offline
  • IslamHouse, HadeethEnc, Qur’an translations and tafsir, Ibn Baz fatwas
  • Read answers aloud with your device’s voices

Adhan and reminders

  • Adhan of Masjid al-Haram or al-Masjid an-Nabawi
  • Reminders 10 and 5 minutes before, spoken in Arabic or English
  • Fajr alarm, Surat Al-Kahf and Jumu’ah on Friday, Ramadan suhoor and iftar

Silent during prayer

  • From the iqama or the Adhan until the prayer ends, for the prayers you choose
  • On Friday, from the Adhan for 45 to 90 minutes
  • Android turns Do Not Disturb on and off, with your permission
  • iPhone and iPad: one tap on the Adhan or iqama notification runs a Shortcut you make once, which turns on Focus until the prayer ends

Widgets and Live Activities

  • 10 widget kinds for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Mac desktop
  • Next-prayer countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Qibla compass in the Dynamic Island
  • 5 styles that colour the app and every widget

Siri and Shortcuts

  • “When is the next prayer in Near Mosque?”
  • “Where is the nearest mosque in Near Mosque?”
  • Directions, Ask, prayer times and the Qibla by voice or the Action button

Apple Watch

  • Next prayer on the living sky
  • Qibla compass with a tap on your wrist
  • Spiritual health: mark your prayers and see your week
  • Tasbih on the Digital Crown
  • 7 complications in 15 sizes

Welcome tour

  • 13 short steps, 12 on Windows and Linux
  • Where you pray, Asr, the Adhan with previews, Friday and the weather sky
  • Your day built from your answers, with a sun you can drag
  • 5 colour styles (Gold and cream, Green, Teal, Night sky, Time of day), light or dark
  • Exactly what leaves your device, then the notification request
  • Show it again from Settings

Every device

  • iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro
  • Apple TV prayer board, full-screen alert and Top Shelf
  • Android phones, foldables and tablets on Google Play, with 8 widget kinds
  • Windows and Linux, downloaded from this page
  • On Windows, the Adhan comes from the notification area

Privacy

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cloud AI
  • Prayer times, Qibla and answers worked out on the device
  • Online only for mosque search, maps, the downloads you choose and the weather sky if you turn it on
