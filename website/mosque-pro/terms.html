The app

Mosque Pro gives you prayer times, the Qibla, nearby mosques, reminders, the Adhan, a library and Ask. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. You may use it for your own personal, non-commercial purposes.

Prayer times and the Qibla

  • Prayer times are calculated from your place and the method you choose. Calculations differ between methods and from local announcements, so follow your local mosque or authority where they differ, above all for Fajr, Maghrib and fasting.
  • The Qibla direction is calculated exactly for your place, but phone compasses are approximate and are disturbed by metal and magnets. Check the direction when it matters.

Ask and the library

  • Ask quotes the books, fatwas and the Qur'an stored on your device and shows where each passage comes from. Where it writes a short answer, it does so from those passages. It is not a fatwa and does not replace a qualified scholar.
  • The answers to common questions are marked as awaiting scholar review until they are reviewed.
  • Translations of the Qur'an are translations of its meanings, credited to their translators.
