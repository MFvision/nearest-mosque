# Recordings in the apps

Converted by `tools/build_audio.py`. The verses and spoken reminders come from two packs supplied by the
project owner (October 2026). The four Adhan recordings were chosen on the owner's request for clean Makkah and
Madinah voices: each candidate was measured (noise in the pauses, signal-to-noise, clipping, completeness) and
the cleanest complete recording per category was kept. Every Adhan and verse is high-passed and normalised to
the same loudness. The source files are not in the repository.

The Adhan recordings first supplied in the owner's pack (Internet Archive MasjidAl-haraamRecordings and the
achaudhry/adhan repository) were replaced because of their background noise (about -34 dBFS) and clipping.

## Use

The project owner chose to use recordings from publicly available sources for this free, ad-free app, which
is not used commercially (owner's statement, 11 October 2026). The notes below record what each source
publishes about rights.

## Rights notes

- The Haramain broadcasts belong to the General Presidency for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques. None of the
  Adhan files carries a licence that can be relied on. The rights holder may ask for permission or removal.
- everyayah.com publishes no licence for its recitations. Many free apps use them.
- The ElevenLabs clips follow the owner's ElevenLabs plan terms.
