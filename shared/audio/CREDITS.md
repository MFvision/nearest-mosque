## Before an App Store or Play release

- The Haramain recordings belong to the General Presidency for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques. None of
  the three Adhan files carries a published licence. Written permission is needed before release.
- everyayah.com publishes no licence for its recitations. Many free apps use them. Confirm with the reciters
  or publishers before release.
- The ElevenLabs clips can be used commercially only on a paid ElevenLabs plan. Check the owner's plan.

## Missing

- A Fajr Adhan from Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, Madinah. The pack's fourth Adhan file is a second Makkah Fajr
  recording (19 July), not Madinah. Until a Madinah Fajr recording is added, the app offers Madinah for the
  Fajr Adhan as "not available yet".
